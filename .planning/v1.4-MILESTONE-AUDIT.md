## Milestone Finding (the result)

The planning A/B is **mixed/inconclusive — itself a valid, honestly-reported finding**:
- **F#** (out-of-distribution FsLex/FsYacc): the expert-authored plan *helped* — Arm A 1/3 reps PASS vs Arm B 0/3. The only F# success came from the Claude plan navigating the build wiring.
- **Scala** (in-distribution): both arms broadly succeed — Arm A 3/3, Arm B 2/3 + 1 PARTIAL. The plan did not change the outcome.
- **Rust** (in-distribution): tie — both 3/3, zero error-fix cycles.
- Net: an expert plan helps mainly when the task is out-of-distribution; for in-distribution work the 35B self-plans just as well. Timing carries a cache/run-order confound and is not read as a planning-quality signal. (No token metrics — P3 dropped; no `usage` in JSONL.)

This extends the standing thesis (capability is domain-distribution, not model size) onto the *planning* axis.
