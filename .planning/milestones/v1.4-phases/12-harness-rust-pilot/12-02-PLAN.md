<jsonl_schema_reference>
Real JSONL schema (confirmed from v1.3 capture): top-level `kind` ("MessageEvent"/"ActionEvent"/"ObservationEvent"/"AgentErrorEvent"/"ConversationErrorEvent"), `source` ("user"/"agent"/"environment"), `timestamp` (ISO8601), `tool_name`, `action` (dict: `kind`="TerminalAction"/"FinishAction", `command`), `observation` (dict: `kind`, `command`, `content`=list of {type,text}, `exit_code`).

Reusable poll/status check (substitute the JSONL path for F):
  python3 -c "
import json,collections
F='<JSONL PATH>'
ev=[json.loads(l) for l in open(F) if l.strip().startswith('{')]
kinds=collections.Counter(e.get('kind') for e in ev)
ta=sum(1 for e in ev if e.get('kind')=='ActionEvent' and e.get('action',{}).get('kind')=='TerminalAction')
finish=any(e.get('kind')=='ActionEvent' and e.get('action',{}).get('kind')=='FinishAction' for e in ev)
err=any(e.get('kind')=='ConversationErrorEvent' for e in ev)
nonzero=sum(1 for e in ev if e.get('kind')=='ObservationEvent' and (e.get('observation') or {}).get('exit_code',0)!=0)
last=ev[-1].get('timestamp') if ev else None
print('kinds=',dict(kinds),'| TerminalActions=',ta,'| FinishAction=',finish,'| ConvError=',err,'| NonZeroExits=',nonzero,'| last_ts=',last)
"
</jsonl_schema_reference>
